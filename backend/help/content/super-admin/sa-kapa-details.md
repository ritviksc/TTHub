## Editing a kapa

Open **Kapa** and select one. Select **Edit details** to change its name or region, then **Save changes**.

## Deleting a kapa

In edit mode, select **Delete branch** and confirm. A kapa can only be deleted when nothing is attached to it: no staff (including deactivated staff), no kaumātua (including inactive ones), no sessions, feedback, physical assessments or saved reports. If anything is attached, a message lists what to move or remove first. In practice, a kapa that has been used cannot be deleted.

## Assigning branch managers and kaimahi

The **Branch managers** and **Instructors** panels list the kapa's staff. To add someone, choose them in the **Assign** list and select **Assign**. Assigning:

- changes their role to match the panel, so it can promote a kaimahi to branch manager or the other way round
- moves them off any other kapa

A kapa can have more than one branch manager. Deactivated staff and super admins cannot be assigned.

Select **Remove** to take someone off the kapa. Their account is unchanged but they are no longer assigned to any kapa.

## Reporting quarters

The **Reporting quarters** panel sets the dates used when choosing a quarter in this kapa's reports. Choose the year, expand **Edit this branch's quarter dates**, adjust Q1 to Q4, and select **Save quarter dates**. **Fill calendar defaults** fills in January–March, April–June, July–September and October–December. All four quarters are needed, each must fall inside the year and quarters cannot overlap, though gaps are allowed. Saved reports keep the dates they were made with.

## Documents and participants

The **Documents** panel is the same file explorer as the Documents page, for this kapa: create folders, upload, download, rename, move and delete. The **Participants** panel lists the kapa's kaumātua and how many are active; open **Kaumātua** to see a person's profile.
