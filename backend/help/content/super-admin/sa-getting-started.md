## The menu

Super admins work nationally and land on **Overview** after signing in. The menu contains:

- **Overview**: national branch, active participant and staffing counts.
- **Sessions**: view all sessions, filter by branch, create sessions for a selected branch and mark and review attendance registers.
- **Kapa**: add or edit branches, assign staff, set reporting quarters and manage branch documents.
- **Kaumātua**: enrol and manage participants across all branches, including reactivation, consent, physical assessments and attendance history.
- **Reports**: attendance and physical statistics, ACC draft preparation and past reports.
- **Feedback**: view and record feedback nationally, with branch, consent and search filters.
- **Administration**: open **Users**, **Audit log** or **Documents**.
- **Help**: these articles and the help assistant.

Your account menu at the top right contains **Log out** and the theme choices.

## Supporting staff

You can read all Branch Manager and Kaimahi help articles. Their branch-specific instructions describe the actions those roles can perform. Super admins can create sessions, enter attendance mode, review absence reasons, finalise and reopen registers for any branch, including sessions created by other staff. Branch managers and instructors perform those actions only within their own kapa. All roles must reopen a finalised register before changing present/absent status. Super admins cannot delete sessions.
