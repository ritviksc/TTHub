## The national kaumātua list

Open **Kaumātua**. Search by participant or kapa name, filter by **All**, **Active** or **Inactive**, and choose a kapa from the branch list. Select a row to open the person's profile.

## Enrolling a kaumātua

Select **Add participant**. Enter their first and last name (required), preferred name, kapa (required), phone and email, then select **Add participant**. They are added to every new session at that kapa from then on.

## The profile

A super admin's profile page works like a branch manager's:

- edit their details and select **Save participant**
- record **GP consent**: tick **Consent recorded**, enter the date given, and optionally attach a supporting document (PDF, JPG or PNG up to 10 MB). Attaching a file does not record consent by itself. Use **Download GP document** to open it.
- record **Photo consent** before adding a photo
- upload, download, rename and delete documents
- start or continue a physical assessment
- see their **Attendance history**

A kaumātua cannot be moved to another kapa from their profile, and there is no reactivate or delete button. **Deactivate participant** keeps their history but stops them being added to new sessions.
