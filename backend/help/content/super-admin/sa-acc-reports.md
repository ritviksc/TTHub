## 1. Choose the period and load feedback

Open **Reporting** → **Prepare ACC report**. Set **From** and **To** (both included), choose a kapa or all branches, or use a quarter. Select **Load feedback**.

Only feedback with recorded reporting consent is listed, 20 per page. If there is none, the page says no consented feedback was recorded for that period.

## 2. Select and review feedback

Tick the feedback to include. Your selection stays as you move between pages. For each selected item:

- choose **Who was speaking?**: Unknown, Participant, Whānau or Kaimahi
- edit the **Reporting copy** to remove identifying details. The app does not anonymise text for you.
- use **Remove from draft** to drop it

You can select up to 100 items, each up to 4,000 characters and 40,000 characters in total.

## 3. Approve and generate

Tick the confirmation that the selected copies are approved for ACC reporting and AI processing. Any change to the selection or text clears the tick, so tick it again after editing. Choose an **AI model** and select **Generate ACC draft**.

The AI receives only the reviewed copies and summary statistics, never names, contact details or individual assessments. If consent was withdrawn meanwhile, reload the feedback.

## 4. Review the draft

The draft has **Recorded participation**, **Recorded physical testing**, an editable **Feedback and delivery narrative**, the **Saved feedback sources** (with the original feedback, which was not sent to the AI) and **Report limitations**. Check every statement against its sources.

Saving edits does not approve the report. The app has no export or submit button and never sends anything to ACC. Find drafts later in **View past ACC reports**.
