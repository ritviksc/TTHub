## 1. Choose the period and load feedback

Open **Reports** → **Prepare ACC report**. Set **From** and **To** (both included), choose a kapa or all branches, or use a quarter. Select **Load feedback**.

Only feedback with recorded reporting consent is listed, 20 per page. If there is none, the page says no consented feedback was recorded for that period.

## 2. Select and review feedback

Tick the feedback to include. Your selection stays as you move between pages. For each selected item:

Expand **Review selected text**, then the item's branch/date row to review or edit it. Both start collapsed to keep the page compact; collapsing them preserves your edits. The approval checkbox remains visible below the review.

- check the displayed speaker type, carried automatically from the recorded feedback: Kaumātua, Kaimahi, Whānau or Unknown; no report-time classification is needed
- edit the **Reporting copy** to remove identifying details. The app does not anonymise text for you.
- use **Remove from draft** to drop it

You can select up to 100 items, each up to 4,000 characters and 40,000 characters in total.

## 3. Approve and generate

Tick the confirmation that the selected copies are approved for ACC reporting and AI processing. Any change to the selection or text clears the tick, so tick it again after editing. Choose an **AI model** and select **Generate ACC draft**. Staff record **Who gave this feedback?** when entering new feedback; older entries without a recorded type show Unknown, and the app does not guess from the quote or recording staff member.

The AI receives only the reviewed copies and summary statistics, never names, contact details or individual assessments. If consent was withdrawn meanwhile, reload the feedback.

## 4. Review the draft

The draft starts with **Engagement**, a **Participation** table with Entity, Location, # Participants, # New Participants, and % Engagement for every branch in the selected scope. Choose **All branches** to include every branch, even those with no feedback or attendance.

Participants counts currently active registrations joined by the end date (unknown joined dates included). New participants counts registrations with recorded joined dates in the inclusive period, including registrations now inactive. Counts use current branch membership and active status; historical transfers and exit dates are not fully recorded. Location uses the branch's recorded region, or **Not recorded** when missing. Engagement is attendance rate: attendances divided by roster places across finalised sessions, multiplied by 100, rounded to the nearest 5 percentage points (85.06% becomes 85%; 87.5% becomes 90%). Incomplete registers are excluded. **Not available** means there is no attendance denominator; a displayed **0%** can also reflect a small rate below 2.5% after rounding. The LLM receives these calculated figures and their definitions; it does not calculate or invent them.

The report also includes **Recorded physical testing**, an editable **Feedback and delivery narrative**, the **Saved feedback sources** (with the original feedback, which was not sent to the AI) and **Report limitations**. Check every statement against its sources. Older drafts retain their original snapshot; generate a new draft to include the branch participation table.

In newly generated narratives, each category lists its own relevant **Supporting quotations**, followed by its **Summary**. Quotations must match the reviewed feedback exactly and be referenced by that section. Generate a fresh draft to use this layout; older saved narratives and your edits keep their existing wording.

Save or cancel any narrative edits, then select **Download PDF**. A clean report opens in a new tab and the browser's print dialog appears; choose **Save as PDF** (or your system's PDF option). The PDF includes the saved narrative, statistics, reviewed feedback and limitations. Original unredacted feedback and editing controls are excluded. If the new tab is blocked, allow pop-ups for this site and try again.

Saving edits or a PDF does not approve the report or send it to ACC. Find drafts later in **View past ACC reports**.
