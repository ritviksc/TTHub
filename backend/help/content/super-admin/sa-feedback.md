## Finding feedback

Open **Feedback**. Choose All branches or a branch, then combine that choice with **All**, **With quote consent**, **Without consent** and the search field. Entries show their quote, related kaumātua or session, speaker type, date, recording staff member, branch and consent status.

## Recording feedback as a super admin

Select **+ New entry**. Choose a Branch first; the current branch filter is preselected when available. Enter the **Quote / short story**, choose **Who gave this feedback?** (Kaumātua, Kaimahi, Whānau or Unknown), and choose a **Related kaumātua**, a **Session**, or both. At least one is required, and both must belong to the chosen branch. Changing branch clears those subject selections while retaining your text.

Check the Date recorded (today or earlier), tick consent only when it is recorded for reporting, and select **Save entry**. The author and branch are recorded automatically. A failed request retains the draft for correction or retry.

## Using feedback in reports

Only consented feedback in the selected dates and branch scope is offered in ACC report preparation. The recorded speaker type carries through automatically; it is not inferred from the author or quote. Review the selected copies, redact identifying details where needed and approve them for ACC reporting and AI processing before generating a draft.

Saved feedback cannot currently be edited or deleted in the app. Check the text, subject, speaker type, date and consent before saving. A session with saved feedback cannot be deleted.
