## Choosing a report

Open **Reports**. There are three cards: **ACC reporting**, **Attendance reporting** and **Physical-testing reporting**. Each has a link to the statistics or draft, and a link to past reports.

## Choosing the period and kapa

Dates start at the last three months. Change **From** and **To** (both dates included), pick a kapa or **All branches**, or choose a year and quarter and select **Use Q… dates**. All branches always uses calendar quarters. Then select **Refresh statistics**. The period can be at most ten years.

## Attendance statistics

The tiles show finalised, incomplete and empty finalised sessions, total attendances, distinct attendees, attendance rate and average per finalised session, with a **Monthly attendance** table. Only finalised registers count. **Show demo data** switches to made-up figures for demonstrations; **Return to live data** switches back.

## Physical testing statistics

The tiles show eligible participants, how many completed testing, completed and draft assessments. **Testing by participant** lets you find a person and **View results**. There is an **Exercise summary** table and a **Monthly recorded measurements** chart.

## AI explanations

Choose an **AI model** (select **Refresh models** if the list is empty) and select **Analyse attendance** or **Analyse physical testing**. Attendance needs at least one finalised session; physical testing needs at least one completed assessment. The request waits in a queue ("queued — waiting for a worker…", then "Generating…") and expires after about 15 minutes.

Super admins can change the generated text with **Edit report**. Saved reports appear in past reports.
