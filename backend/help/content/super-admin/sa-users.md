## The users list

Open **Users**. Filter by role with **All**, **Branch Managers**, **Instructors** or **Super Admins**, and by kapa with the branch list. The table shows each person's name, email, role, kapa and whether they are active. Your own account is not listed, so you cannot edit or deactivate yourself.

## Adding a staff account

1. Select **Add user**.
2. Enter their first name, last name and email. Staff sign in with their email, and each email can only be used once.
3. Enter a password of at least 8 characters. Give it to the person yourself: the app does not send invitation emails.
4. Choose the role: **Branch Manager**, **Instructor** (kaimahi) or **Super Admin**.
5. For a branch manager or kaimahi, choose their kapa. Super admins never have a kapa, so the field is hidden.
6. Select **Create account**. "Account created successfully." confirms it; **Add another** starts a new one.

If you only see "Failed to create account.", check that the email is not already used and that a kapa is chosen.

## Passwords

There is no password reset in the app, and staff cannot change their own password. Choose a password carefully when you create the account.
