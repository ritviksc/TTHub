## The Administration page

Open **Administration**. Only **Audit log** is available. The Vocabularies and Exports cards are not in use yet.

## Reading the audit log

Select **Audit log**. Super admins see activity from every kapa. Filter by staff email, activity (Staff changes, Branch settings, Reports, Record updates, Sign-ins), kapa, result (Successful, Could not complete, Not permitted) and a date range, then select **Show activity**. The table shows when, who, what happened, the kapa and the result, newest first.

The log records staff and kapa changes, quarter changes, report requests and edits, sign-ins and changes to records. It is read-only: entries cannot be edited or deleted. It never stores passwords, AI prompts or report text.
