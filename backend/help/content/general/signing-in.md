## Signing in

Select **Login** on the home page and sign in with your work email and password. Your account and password are set up for you: super admins create all accounts, and branch managers create kaimahi accounts.

After signing in you go to your starting page: **Overview** for super admins, **Overview** for branch managers and **Home** for kaimahi.

## Forgotten passwords

The app has no password reset, and nobody can change a password in the app, including super admins. If you have forgotten yours, contact a super admin (kaimahi can ask their branch manager to pass this on) so they can arrange it with whoever runs the system.

## Signing out

Open your account menu at the top right of any page and select **Log out**. If your session expires, you are returned to the home page and need to sign in again.

## Deactivated accounts

A deactivated account can no longer sign in. A super admin can reactivate staff accounts from **Administration → Users**. Branch managers can reactivate instructors at their own kapa from **Administration → Kaimahi**. Contact the appropriate administrator if you think yours was deactivated by mistake.
