## Names for people and groups

- **Kaumātua** are the participants (elders) who attend Taurite Tū classes. Some screens may also say pakeke (adults). "Participant" means the same thing.
- **Kaimahi** are the instructors who run sessions. "Instructor" means the same thing.
- **Kapa** are the branches. Each branch has its own kaumātua, kaimahi, sessions and branch manager. "Branch" means the same thing.
- **Whānau** means family, and here also the wider community that comes along to classes.

## Staff roles

- **Super Admin** works nationally. They manage staff accounts and kapa, and can see every kapa's records and reports.
- **Branch Manager** manages one kapa: its sessions, kaumātua, kaimahi, feedback and reports.
- **Kaimahi (instructor)** runs sessions for their kapa, takes attendance and records physical assessments.
