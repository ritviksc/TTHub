## Finding feedback at your kapa

Open **Feedback** to see recorded quotes and short stories. Filter by All, With quote consent or Without consent, or search by quote or participant. Each entry identifies the related kaumātua or session, speaker type, date, recording staff member and reporting consent.

## Recording feedback as kaimahi

Select **+ New entry** and enter the **Quote / short story**. Select **Who gave this feedback?**: Kaumātua, Kaimahi, Whānau or Unknown. This describes the original speaker, separately from the participant or session the feedback relates to.

Choose a **Related kaumātua**, a **Session**, or both from your kapa. At least one is required. Check Date recorded (today or earlier), tick consent only when recorded for reporting, then select **Save entry**. Your account is recorded as the author. Failed saves retain the draft for retry.

## Consent and saved entries

Only feedback with reporting consent is offered for ACC preparation by super admins. Older entries without a speaker type remain Unknown. Saved feedback cannot currently be edited or deleted, so check the text and consent before saving. A session with saved feedback cannot be deleted.
