## Opening a profile

Open **Kaumātua** and select a name. The **Details** and **Attendance history** buttons open separate pages. The Details page has the person's physical assessments, their details form, consent, documents and status.

## Editing details

In **Participant details** you can change their first and last name (both required), preferred name, date of birth, email, phone, emergency contact name and phone, date joined, and general notes. General notes are not a clinical record. Select **Save participant** to save; "Participant saved." confirms it.

## Photo consent and photos

To add a photo, first tick **Consent recorded** under photo consent and enter the **Date given** (it cannot be in the future). The **Participant photo** picker only works once consent is ticked. The photo is shown only while photo consent is recorded. Select **Save participant** to save.

## GP consent

Kaimahi can see whether GP consent is recorded ("Consent recorded — date" or "Consent not recorded"), but cannot record it or open the GP consent document. Ask your branch manager or a super admin to record GP consent.

## Documents on a profile

In the **Documents** section, choose a file and select **Upload**. You can upload PDF, JPG, PNG, Word or Excel files up to 10 MB. Kaimahi can **Download** documents. Only branch managers and super admins can rename or delete them.

## Deactivating a kaumātua

If someone has stopped attending, use **Deactivate participant** in the **Participant status** section, then **Confirm deactivation**. They are no longer added to new sessions, and their attendance history and results are kept.

## Reactivating a kaumātua

Open the inactive person’s **Details** page. In **Participant status**, select **Reactivate participant**, then **Confirm reactivation**. They can be included in new sessions again. Their saved history is preserved, and existing session rosters are not changed. All staff roles can reactivate participants they are allowed to access.
