## What the session page shows

Select a session in **Sessions** to open it. The page shows:

- the activity, date and time
- whether attendance is finalised ("Attendance finalised" or "Attendance not finalised")
- tiles for **Present**, **Absent** and **Attendance rate**
- a table of everyone on the roster, with their status and absence reason

Use the filters **All**, **Present** and **Absent** to narrow the table. Select **‹ All sessions** to go back to the list.

## Changing attendance from the table

While the register is not finalised, you can change a person's status (Present or Absent) and absence reason straight from the table. Changes save immediately. For **Other**, type a description; the reason is saved once there is text.

When the register is finalised these controls are greyed out. Select **Review attendance** and then **Reopen attendance** to make corrections.
