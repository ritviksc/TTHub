## The menu

The menu at the top of every page shows your kapa's name and these items:

- **Home**: your starting page.
- **Sessions**: create sessions and take attendance.
- **Kaumātua**: the participants enrolled at your kapa, their details, attendance history and physical assessments.
- **Feedback**: record and find quotes or short stories related to a kaumātua or session, with the speaker type and reporting consent.
- **Documents**: organisation files and your kapa's files.
- **Help**: this page.

You only see records for your own kapa.

## The Home page

The Home page loads your kapa’s real sessions. It features a session today, the next upcoming session, or a recent session. **Start roll call** opens attendance mode and **Open session details** opens the register. Recent sessions and links to kaumātua, the timetable and documents are also available. If sessions do not load, refresh the page or open **Sessions**.

## What kaimahi can and cannot do

Kaimahi can:

- create sessions and take attendance
- edit a kaumātua's details and photo consent, and upload documents to their profile
- record physical assessments
- enrol a new kaumātua at your kapa
- deactivate or reactivate a kaumātua
- record qualitative feedback and reporting consent

Kaimahi cannot:

- record GP consent or open GP consent documents
- rename or delete documents
- see other kapa
