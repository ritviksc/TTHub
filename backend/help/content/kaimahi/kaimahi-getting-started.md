## The menu

The menu at the top of every page shows your kapa's name and these items:

- **Home**: your starting page.
- **Sessions**: create sessions and take attendance.
- **Kaumātua**: the participants enrolled at your kapa, their details, attendance history and physical assessments.
- **Feedback**: not available yet. The page says "This page is coming soon."
- **Documents**: organisation files and your kapa's files.
- **Help**: this page.

You only see records for your own kapa.

## The Home page

The Home page is a preview of a future dashboard. It shows demonstration data only: the sessions, names and alerts on it are not real, and its buttons (such as **Start roll call** and **Open session details**) do not do anything yet. Use **Sessions** to work with real sessions.

## What kaimahi can and cannot do

Kaimahi can:

- create sessions and take attendance
- edit a kaumātua's details and photo consent, and upload documents to their profile
- record physical assessments
- deactivate a kaumātua

Kaimahi cannot:

- enrol a new kaumātua (a super admin does this)
- reactivate a kaumātua
- record GP consent or open GP consent documents
- rename or delete documents
- see other kapa
