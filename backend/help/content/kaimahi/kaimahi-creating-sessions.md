## Finding sessions

Open **Sessions** in the menu. The table lists your kapa's sessions with their activity, date, time and attendance (for example "✓ 12 / 14 present"). Use the filters **This month**, **This quarter** or **All** to narrow the list. Select a row to open the session.

## Creating a new session

1. Open **Sessions** and select **+ New session** (or **+ Create session** if there are no sessions yet).
2. In the **Create new session** dialog, choose the date, the time and the activity: **Standard strength and mobility training** or **Fitness test**. All three are required.
3. Select **Create session**. The new session opens straight away.

The date can be today or later, but not in the past ("Session date cannot be in the past.").

Only kaimahi can create sessions. Branch managers and super admins can view sessions but not create them.

## Who is on the roster

When a session is created, every active kaumātua at your kapa is added to its roster and marked absent until you mark them present. Kaumātua enrolled after the session was created are not added to it, and inactive kaumātua are never added.

## Deleting a session

Open the session and select **Delete session**, then confirm. This cannot be undone. Only the kaimahi who created the session can delete it, and a session that has saved feedback cannot be deleted.
