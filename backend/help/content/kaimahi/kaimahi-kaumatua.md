## The kaumātua list

Open **Kaumātua** in the menu to see everyone enrolled at your kapa. Each row shows the person's photo or initial, their name (their preferred name, with their full name underneath if it is different) and whether they are **Active** or **Inactive**. The total count is at the top right.

Type in **Search participants...** to find someone by preferred or full name. If nothing matches, select **Show all participants** to clear the search.

Select a name to open that person's profile.

## Enrolling someone new

Kaimahi cannot enrol a new kaumātua in the app. Only a super admin can enrol people, so ask your branch manager to arrange it with a super admin. Once they are enrolled and active, they are added to every new session you create.
