## Viewing attendance history

Open **Kaumātua**, select a name, then select the **Attendance history** tab. You see tiles for **Present**, **Absent** and **Attendance rate**, and a table of each session with its date and time, activity, attendance and absence reason.

The history is read-only. To correct an absence reason, open the session and select Review attendance. To change present/absent status, reopen the register, correct it and select Done (see Taking attendance).

## Why some sessions are not counted

Only finalised registers count. If a session's register was never finished with **Done**, it is left out of the totals, and the page says how many unfinished register entries were excluded. Only sessions at your kapa are shown.
