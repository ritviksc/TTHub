## Finding and creating sessions

Open **Sessions** and switch between **Calendar** and **List**. The list has **This month**, **This quarter** and **All** filters. Select a session to see its register.

Select **+ New session**, or select a calendar day and use the blue **+ Add session** button. Choose a date today or later, time and activity, then select **Create session**. Your kapa is assigned automatically, and its active participants form the roster. People enrolled or reactivated later are included in new sessions only; existing rosters do not change.

## Taking attendance

Open a session and select **Attendance mode**. Tap a participant’s card to mark them present; tap again to mark them absent. Cards show the photo when photo consent is recorded, or an initial. No absence-reason rows appear while marking attendance.

Select **Done** to finalise and return to the session details. If nobody is present, confirm the empty register. Only finalised registers count in attendance reports.

## Reviewing and correcting

Select **Review attendance** on a finalised session to edit reasons for absent participants. Choose No show, Medical appointment, Transport issue or Other; Other requires a description. **Save reason** saves one participant’s reason and stays in review. **Done** saves pending reason edits and returns to session details without reopening the register.

To change present/absent status, select **Reopen attendance**. Pending reasons save first, then the simple participant cards return with no reason rows. Make the corrections and select **Done** to finalise again.

## Following up unfinished registers

The **Overview** page links to sessions awaiting finalisation. You can check and finish these registers yourself, or ask the kaimahi who ran the class. Branch managers can create sessions and manage attendance at their own kapa, but cannot delete sessions. Deletion is limited to the instructor who created a session, and saved feedback prevents deletion.
