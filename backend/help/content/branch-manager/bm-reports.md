## Opening a report

Open **Reports** and choose **View attendance statistics** or **View physical-testing statistics**. Reports always cover your own kapa.

## Choosing the dates

Dates start at the last three months. Change **From** and **To** (both included), or choose a **Year** and **Quarter** and select **Use Q… dates**. Then select **Refresh statistics**. The period can be at most ten years.

**Show demo data** shows made-up figures for demonstrations; they are never saved or sent to the AI. **Return to live data** switches back.

## Attendance statistics

Tiles show finalised, incomplete and empty finalised sessions, total attendances, distinct attendees, attendance rate and average per finalised session. The **Monthly attendance** table shows each month and the change in rate. Only finalised registers count. **How to read this report** explains the limits.

## Physical testing statistics

Tiles show eligible participants, how many completed testing, and completed and draft assessments. **Testing by participant** lets you find someone and select **View results** to see their first and latest results. The **Exercise summary** and **Monthly recorded measurements** chart show the kapa overall.

## AI explanations

Choose an **AI model** (select **Refresh models** if none are listed) and select **Analyse attendance** or **Analyse physical testing**. Attendance needs at least one finalised session and physical testing needs at least one completed assessment. Only aggregate statistics are sent to the AI. The request waits in a queue, then generates; changing the model clears the explanation.

Branch managers cannot edit an explanation or open past reports. Super admins can.
