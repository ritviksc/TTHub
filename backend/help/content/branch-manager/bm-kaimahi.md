## The kaimahi list

Open **Kaimahi** to see the instructors at your kapa. Search by name or email, and filter by **All**, **Active** or **Inactive**. The table shows each person's email, status and **Sessions led** (how many sessions they created).

## Adding a kaimahi

1. Select **+ Add Kaimahi**.
2. Enter their first name, last name and email address.
3. Enter a password of at least 8 characters. They use it to sign in, so give it to them yourself: the app does not send invitation emails and has no password reset.
4. Select **Create instructor**.

The new account is a kaimahi at your kapa. Branch managers can only create kaimahi accounts. Each email can only be used once ("An account with this email already exists.").

## Editing a kaimahi

Select their name, then **Edit details**. You can change their first name, last name and email (their sign-in username), then **Save changes**. Only a super admin can change someone's role or kapa.

## Deactivating a kaimahi

Select **Deactivate** in the list, or on their page, and confirm. They can no longer sign in, and the sessions they ran are kept. There is no reactivate button, so ask a super admin if you deactivated someone by mistake.
