## Opening the audit log

Open **Administration** and select **Open audit log →**. You see activity related to your kapa only, including changes affecting staff who have left it.

## Filtering

Filter by staff email, activity (Staff changes, Branch settings, Reports, Record updates, Sign-ins), result (Successful, Could not complete, Not permitted) and a date range, then select **Show activity**. Entries are shown newest first in New Zealand time, with **Previous** and **Next** to page through them.

## What is recorded

The log records who did what and when: staff changes, quarter changes, reports requested, sign-ins, and changes to kaumātua, sessions, attendance, physical assessments and documents. It is read-only, and it never stores passwords, AI prompts or report text. Asking the help assistant a question is not recorded.
