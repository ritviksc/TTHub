## The kaumātua list

Open **Kaumātua** to see everyone enrolled at your kapa, with **Active** or **Inactive** status. Search by preferred or full name and select a name to open their profile.

There is no enrol button for branch managers. A super admin enrols new kaumātua.

## Editing a profile

On the profile you can edit the person's details (name, preferred name, date of birth, contact details, emergency contact, date joined and general notes) and select **Save participant**. General notes are not a clinical record.

## Recording GP consent

Only branch managers and super admins can record GP consent.

1. Tick **Consent recorded** under **GP consent**.
2. Enter the **Date given**. It is required and cannot be in the future.
3. Optionally attach a **Supporting document** (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB). Attaching a file does not record consent by itself; the tick does.
4. Select **Save participant**.

Use **Download GP document** to open the attached file, or **Replace supporting document** to change it. The **Overview** page shows how many kaumātua are missing GP consent.

## Photo consent and documents

Tick **Consent recorded** under **Photo consent** and enter the date before attaching a **Participant photo**. The photo is shown only while photo consent is recorded.

In **Documents** on the profile, upload PDF, JPG, PNG, Word or Excel files up to 10 MB. Branch managers can download, rename and delete them.

## Deactivating

**Deactivate participant** then **Confirm deactivation** stops someone being added to new sessions and keeps their history and results. It cannot be undone in the app.
