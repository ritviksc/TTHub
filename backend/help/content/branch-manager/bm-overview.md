## The overview tiles

**Overview** opens with a summary of your kapa: how many participants, sessions and kaimahi it has. The tiles show:

- **Active participants**, with the total ever enrolled
- **Average attendance** for finalised sessions in the current calendar quarter ("—" when there is no data)
- **Sessions held**, with how many are not finalised yet
- **Instructors**

## Needs attention

This list points you at things to follow up:

- kaumātua missing GP consent (**View participants →**)
- sessions waiting for their register to be finalised (**Review session →**). Remind the kaimahi who ran it to finish attendance with **Done**.
- a reminder that the quarterly attendance and physical report can be generated (**Open reports →**)

## Charts and recent sessions

**Attendance by finalised session** charts the attendance rate of the last 10 finalised sessions. **Recent sessions** lists the last five, with how many attended and whether each is **Finalised** or **Draft**. Use **Full report →** and **All sessions →** for more.

The **Reporting quarters** card is also here; see Setting your kapa's reporting quarters.
