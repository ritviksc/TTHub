## What reporting quarters are

Each kapa can have its own four reporting periods for a year. They are used when you choose a quarter in a report. If none are saved for a year, calendar quarters are used: January–March, April–June, July–September and October–December.

## Changing the dates

Open **Overview** and find the **Reporting quarters** card, or use the quarter settings on an attendance or physical testing report.

1. Choose the **Year**.
2. Expand **Edit this branch's quarter dates**.
3. Set the start and end date of Q1 to Q4. **Fill calendar defaults** fills in the calendar quarters for you to adjust.
4. Select **Save quarter dates**. "Quarter dates saved for …" confirms it.

## Rules

All four quarters are needed. Every date must be inside the chosen year, each quarter must start on or before it ends, and quarters must be in order without overlapping. Gaps are allowed. Quarters that cross into another year are not supported.

Saved AI reports keep the dates they were made with.
