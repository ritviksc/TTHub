## Viewing feedback

Open **Feedback** to see kaumātua, kaimahi and whānau voices, stories and quotes recorded at your kapa. Each entry shows the quote, its related kaumātua or session, the recorded speaker type, date, recording staff member, and either **Consent ✓** or **Not for reporting**. Filter by **With quote consent** or **Without consent**, or search quotes and participants.

## Recording a new entry

1. Select **+ New entry**.
2. Type the **Quote / Feedback text**.
3. Select **Who gave this feedback?**: Kaumātua, Kaimahi, Whānau or Unknown. Record the original speaker's type; choose Unknown if it is not known.
4. Choose the **Related kaumātua**, the **Session**, or both. At least one is required, and they must be from your kapa. These identify what the feedback relates to; the speaker type is recorded separately.
5. Enter the **Date recorded**. It cannot be in the future.
6. Tick the consent box only when consent is recorded for this feedback to be included in reports.
7. Select **Save entry**.

## Consent and reports

Only feedback with consent ticked can be used in ACC reports, which super admins prepare. The recorded speaker type carries into reports automatically, so reviewers do not need to classify it again. Older entries without a recorded type remain Unknown. Feedback cannot be edited or deleted after saving, so check the text, speaker type and consent before you save. A session with saved feedback cannot be deleted.
