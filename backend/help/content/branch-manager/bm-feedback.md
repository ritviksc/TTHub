## Viewing feedback

Open **Feedback** to see participant voice, stories and quotes recorded at your kapa. Each entry shows the quote, who it came from, the date, who recorded it, and either **Consent ✓** or **Not for reporting**. Filter by **With quote consent** or **Without consent**, or search quotes and participants.

## Recording a new entry

1. Select **+ New entry**.
2. Type the **Quote / Feedback text**.
3. Choose the **Participant** it came from, the **Session** it was given at, or both. At least one is required, and they must be from your kapa.
4. Enter the **Date recorded**. It cannot be in the future.
5. Tick the consent box only if the participant has agreed for this quote to be included in reports.
6. Select **Save entry**.

## Consent and reports

Only feedback with consent ticked can be used in ACC reports, which super admins prepare. Feedback cannot be edited or deleted after saving, so check the text and consent before you save. A session with saved feedback cannot be deleted.
